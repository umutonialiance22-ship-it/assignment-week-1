Chocolate Pancake Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons cocoa powder

Preparation

Mix the ingredients and cook the pancakes on a hot pan.

Recipe Information

Preparation time: 10 minutes

Cooking time: 15 minutes

Servings: 4 people

Preparation Steps

  1. Mix the flour and cocoa powder.
  2. Add the milk and eggs.
  3. Mix until the batter is smooth.
  4. Heat a pan over medium heat.
  5. Cook each pancake for a few minutes on both sides.
  6. Serve and enjoy!
